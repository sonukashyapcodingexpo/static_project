Charge Entry

Charge entry is the step between the visit and the coder. A missing note is a hold. A guessed unit becomes a denial, so it is not entered.

Request Project Quote Schedule Consultation
Desk Overview

How the work is run

  • Charges come from the encounter form and the signed note
  • Missing documentation is held, not invented
  • Units, modifiers, and place of service are entered as their own fields
  • A duplicate charge is caught before it is billed
The Operating Problem

The visit that never becomes a charge is lost revenue

Lagging charge entry hides visits in a stack of superbills. Entering them without the note creates a different problem: a claim that cannot be defended. The queue does both — nothing waits in a pile, and nothing is entered blind.

Scope & Deliverables

What this desk takes on

The work is a queue with an owner, a note, and a next action. These are the pieces that queue contains.

Superbill and encounter capture

Every visit on the schedule has a charge, a hold, or a reason it was not billed.

  • The encounter form matched to the appointment
  • Diagnoses and procedures entered as coded fields
  • A visit with no form chased the same day

Holds for missing documentation

A charge without a signed note does not move to billing.

  • The missing item named on the hold
  • The provider who still owes the note
  • A daily list of holds older than the limit you set

Units, modifiers, and place of service

The fields payers deny are entered on purpose.

  • Units taken from the order, not assumed
  • Laterality and assistant modifiers when the note has them
  • Place of service matched to where the visit happened

Duplicate and overlap checks

The same procedure is not entered twice for the same day.

  • Same patient, same date, same code flagged
  • A professional charge kept distinct from a facility charge
  • The coder told when two encounters disagree

Handoff to coding

Thin charts go to a coder query instead of a finished claim.

  • A complete charge moves on
  • An incomplete charge is queried
  • The coder sees the source form beside the note

Charge lag, counted daily

A visit still unbilled after the cutoff is visible.

  • Lag by provider and by location
  • The oldest unbilled visit at the top
  • A number you can compare week to week

Methodology

How a day on this desk runs

Four steps, kept visible, so a claim does not sit between two people.

Match the visit

The appointment and the encounter form are paired. A visit with neither is listed.

Enter the charge

Codes, units, modifiers, and place of service are keyed from the form and the note.

Hold or release

Complete charges move to coding and billing. Missing notes stay on a hold with an owner.

Count the lag

Unbilled visits are reported by age so a stack cannot hide at the back of the desk.
