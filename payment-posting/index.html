Payment Posting

Posting is how the deposit becomes an account that is actually closed. Contractual write-offs, real underpayments, and patient responsibility are not the same adjustment.

Request Project Quote Schedule Consultation
Desk Overview

How the work is run

  • ERA and paper EOBs posted to the claim they belong to
  • A contractual adjustment is not used to hide an underpayment
  • Patient copays and card payments land on the right date of service
  • The posted total is reconciled to the deposit
The Operating Problem

A balanced deposit can still hide an underpaid claim

If every difference is written off as contractual, the payer’s short pay never gets called. Posting separates what the contract allows from what is still owed.

Scope & Deliverables

What this desk takes on

The work is a queue with an owner, a note, and a next action. These are the pieces that queue contains.

ERA and paper EOB posting

Electronic 835 files and scanned EOBs hit the claim, not a side spreadsheet.

  • Line-level paid, adjusted, and patient amounts
  • Unmatched claims held in an exception queue
  • A paper EOB imaged on the claim it paid

Underpayment review

The allowed amount is compared with the contracted rate before anything is written off.

  • Short pays queued for AR, not adjusted away
  • A reason on every variance
  • Recovered dollars reported apart from new payments

Patient payments

Copays, statement payments, and card receipts post to the encounter.

  • The date of service on the receipt
  • Credit balances flagged the day they appear
  • A refund path when the patient paid twice

Adjustments with a reason

Write-offs name the contract, the small balance, or the denial.

  • No blank adjustment codes
  • A second person on write-offs over the threshold you set
  • The denial desk told when the “adjustment” is really a denial

Deposit reconciliation

What the bank received and what was posted are the same number, or the difference is listed.

  • Daily deposit against posted cash
  • Payer takebacks recorded, not ignored
  • A leftover item that cannot be placed stays open

Same-day close

Yesterday’s payments are posted before the AR callers work today’s book.

  • A cutoff time for the day’s files
  • Exceptions cleared or assigned
  • AR sees the balance after posting, not before

Methodology

How a day on this desk runs

Four steps, kept visible, so a claim does not sit between two people.

Take in the file

ERA files and paper EOBs are matched to the deposit.

Post the lines

Paid, adjusted, and patient amounts hit the claim. Unmatched lines wait.

Separate the variance

Contractual write-offs close. Underpayments and denials are queued for AR or appeals.

Reconcile

Posted cash is tied to the deposit, and the open difference is listed before the day ends.
