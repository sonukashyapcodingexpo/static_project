AR Calling

An account does not move because it was dialed. It moves because the call captured the claim status, the missing document, or the payment date, and someone owns the next step.

Request Project Quote Schedule Consultation
Desk Overview

How the work is run

  • Every open account sits in an aging bucket with an owner
  • The note names the reference number, the promise, and the next date
  • Patient calls follow a script and stay on the balance that is actually owed
  • A claim near a filing limit is escalated instead of left in the queue
The Operating Problem

Aged AR grows when the next call has no note

Payers stall on claims that were never re-touched, and patient balances sit because the last call did not say what was owed. The calling desk works the book by age, not by whichever account is on top.

Scope & Deliverables

What this desk takes on

The work is a queue with an owner, a note, and a next action. These are the pieces that queue contains.

Work queues by aging bucket

0–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90-plus are separate queues so the oldest dollars are not buried.

  • A daily list with an owner
  • High-dollar accounts pulled to the top
  • A count of accounts touched against accounts due

Payer follow-up with a reference

The call records the status the representative actually gave.

  • Claim number and call reference written down
  • The missing item named, not “pending”
  • A follow-up date before the account is closed for the day

Patient balance calls

Patients hear the amount, the date of service, and how to pay, without a lecture.

  • A script limited to the balance due
  • Card, statement, and plan options named
  • A dispute routed back to coding instead of argued on the call

Escalation before the filing limit

A claim that is aging toward a deadline does not stay in the ordinary queue.

  • Timely filing dates on the account
  • A supervisor queue for stalled payers
  • A written appeal started when a call will not move it

A note the next caller can use

The next person should not have to call the payer to learn what the last person was told.

  • Status, promise, and date in the same note
  • No account closed without a next action
  • The note visible to coding and posting

A daily production view

Calls only count when the account changed state.

  • Touched, promised, paid, and escalated
  • Dollars worked, not only dials
  • The accounts nobody reached, listed for the next day

Methodology

How a day on this desk runs

Four steps, kept visible, so a claim does not sit between two people.

Book the queue

Open accounts are sorted by payer, age, and dollars, and the day’s list is assigned.

Call and record

The payer or patient is called, and the note stores the status, the reference, and the promise.

Set the next action

Each account gets a date, an owner, or an escalation. Nothing is left as “called.”

Report the book

Touched dollars, promises, and untouched accounts are visible the same day.
