Eligibility Verification

A claim denied for inactive coverage was knowable the day before the visit. Verification writes the benefit on the encounter while the schedule can still change.

Request Project Quote Schedule Consultation
Desk Overview

How the work is run

  • Coverage is checked before the patient arrives
  • Copay, deductible, and coinsurance are written on the encounter
  • Authorization and referral flags stop a visit that will not be paid
  • The coder sees the same benefit note the front desk saw
The Operating Problem

The expensive denial is the one that was visible yesterday

Inactive plans, exhausted visits, and missing authorizations show up on the schedule. Checking them after the claim returns is how that revenue is lost.

Scope & Deliverables

What this desk takes on

The work is a queue with an owner, a note, and a next action. These are the pieces that queue contains.

Active coverage on the schedule

Tomorrow’s patients are checked against the plan on file.

  • Inactive and terminated plans flagged
  • The subscriber and the patient matched
  • A mismatch sent back to registration

Copay, deductible, and coinsurance

The front desk knows what to collect, and the patient is not surprised.

  • The amount due at the visit
  • Remaining deductible when the plan returns it
  • The figure stored on the encounter, not on a sticky note

Authorization and referral flags

A visit that needs an auth is marked before the patient is in the room.

  • Auth required, referral required, or neither
  • The auth number written when it exists
  • A visit held when the number is missing and the plan will not pay

Same-day rechecks

A plan change the morning of the visit is caught again.

  • Recheck when the card in hand does not match
  • A second payer when the first is inactive
  • The result visible to coding the same day

A note the coder can use

Eligibility is part of the chart, not a separate binder.

  • Plan, status, and date of the check
  • The limitation that changes the code or the setting
  • Denied-for-eligibility claims traced to whether a check happened

A queue for the exceptions

Cleared visits need no more work. The exceptions do.

  • Only the failed and the incomplete checks are staffed
  • A reason on each one
  • Registration sees the list before the session starts
Where it is used

Use Case

Methodology

How a day on this desk runs

Four steps, kept visible, so a claim does not sit between two people.

Pull the schedule

The next sessions are listed with the plan and the subscriber already on the patient.

Check the benefit

Active or inactive, copay, deductible, coinsurance, and auth are recorded.

Flag the exception

Inactive plans and missing auths go to registration while the visit can still move.

Leave it on the encounter

Coding and billing read the same note, including the date it was checked.
