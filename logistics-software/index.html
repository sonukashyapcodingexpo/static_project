The Business Challenge

Eliminating Blind Spots in Complex Freight & Dispatch Operations

Fragmented telematics, lost paperwork, and inefficient route dispatch drain freight margins. We engineer custom logistics software that provides end-to-end visibility from initial dispatch to proof-of-delivery.

Scope & Deliverables

What We Build & Deliver

Every engagement produces concrete, documented, and production-tested deliverables.

Intelligent Dispatch & Load Planning

Dynamic route planning optimizing fuel consumption, driver hours, and capacity utilization.

  • Routes respect hours, capacity, and the stops that matter
  • A planner can move a job and see the effect
  • The plan the driver receives is the one dispatch approved

Live Fleet GPS & Telemetry Tracking

Real-time vehicle location, temperature sensor logging, and automated ETA calculation.

  • A vehicle's place and ETA on one map
  • Temperature or sensor alerts where the load needs them
  • A late stop is visible before the customer calls

Digital Proof-of-Delivery (ePOD)

Driver mobile apps capturing sign-on-glass, geofenced timestamps, and photo verification.

  • A signature, a time, and a photo from the drop
  • The proof is tied to the order, not lost in a camera roll
  • A failed delivery has a reason the office can read

Carrier Rate Comparison & Freight Audit

Automated matching of carrier invoices against agreed contract rate sheets.

  • A shipment can be priced against the carriers you use
  • An invoice is checked against the rate you agreed
  • A mismatch is listed for finance instead of paid by habit

The driver and the office see the same job

Dispatch, the map, and the proof of delivery belong to one shipment.

  • A job the driver can open offline
  • Status the customer can be told
  • Exceptions back at the desk the same day

Customers told without a phone chase

An ETA and a delivered state can be shared from the system.

  • A tracking view or a message at the milestones you choose
  • A failed delivery with a reason
  • The same status in the office and in the message
