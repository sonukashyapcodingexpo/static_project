The Business Challenge

Avoiding Duplicate Content Penalties Across Global Markets

Expanding into international territories requires far more than automated translation. Misconfigured hreflang tags and duplicate English content can confuse search engines and cannibalize regional rankings.

Scope & Deliverables

What We Build & Deliver

Every engagement produces concrete, documented, and production-tested deliverables.

International URL Architecture

Advising on ccTLDs, subdirectories (`/uk/`, `/ca/`), or subdomains for optimal authority consolidation.

  • One structure for countries and languages, chosen before the build
  • Authority stays on the domain you want to grow
  • A redirect plan for any old country URLs

Flawless Hreflang Tag Implementation

XML sitemap and header hreflang markup signaling exact regional and linguistic targeting.

  • Each page points at the correct language and region
  • The sitemap and the tags agree
  • A check so a page is not telling Google two different things

Localized Keyword & Intent Research

Targeting regional terminology (e.g. US vs. UK business vocabulary) rather than literal translations.

  • Terms people actually type in that country, not a straight translation
  • Pages mapped to the query, not just translated in bulk
  • A short list of pages to write or revise first

Local Search & Backlink Acquisition

Securing authoritative citations and backlinks from regional country-code domains.

  • Citations and links from sites in that country
  • The local profile matches the page you want to rank
  • A record of what was placed and where it points

A country rollout in an order

The first country is finished before the tenth is started.

  • One region live, with tags and the URL structure
  • What is translated and what is written locally
  • A measurement window before the next country

Reporting by market

A global average does not hide a country that is stuck.

  • Queries and pages for each market
  • Index coverage for the new URLs
  • A note when a tag or a redirect is wrong
