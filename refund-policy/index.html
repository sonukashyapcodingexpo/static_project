Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. What this covers

This policy covers fees paid to Luminate for custom software, websites, mobile apps, and related IT services. A signed proposal or statement of work controls if it says something different about a particular project.

2. Before work starts

If you cancel in writing before kickoff, and no discovery, design, or development time has been used, the unused deposit is refunded in full. Write to us before the agreed start date so the slot can be released.

3. After work has started

Time already spent, and milestones already delivered, are not refundable. If you have paid ahead for work that has not started, that unused portion can be refunded once any open costs are settled. A change of scope is handled as a revision to the plan, not as a refund of completed work.

4. Third-party costs

Domains, hosting, app-store fees, licenses, advertising spend, and other costs paid to a third party are refundable only when that provider returns the money. Luminate does not refund amounts that have already been passed through.

5. How to request a refund

Email hrd@luminabusinesssolutions.com or call +88 (9800) 6802 within 14 days of the invoice or cancellation. Include the project name, the amount, and the reason. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 14 business days.
