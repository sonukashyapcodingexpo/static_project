Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a contact enquiry, project scoping form, or career application via this website, we collect personal data provided directly by you, including your name, email address, company name, phone number (if provided), and project specifications.

2. Purpose of Processing

We process personal data solely to evaluate and respond to commercial project enquiries, prepare engineering proposals, conduct recruitment activities, and communicate updates regarding mutually agreed client contracts. We do not sell or monetize personal information.

3. Data Retention & Security

All submissions are processed through encrypted HTTPS channels and retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the commercial or legal purposes for which they were gathered. We maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards compliant with current ISO and GDPR data security standards.

4. Third-Party Integrations

Our static website does not execute invasive third-party tracking scripts. When external forms, analytics, or hosting infrastructure (e.g. cloud DNS) are used, they adhere to compliant Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).

5. Your Rights

Depending on your regional jurisdiction (such as GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in California), you have rights to access, rectify, or request deletion of your personal data. For all data privacy inquiries, contact our data protection officer at privacy@company.com.
