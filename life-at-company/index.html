Engineering Culture

Built by Engineers, for Engineers

We believe great software is built by rested, autonomous engineers who have the time and focus to solve hard problems without meeting overload or bureaucratic friction.

Our Manifesto

How We Work Daily

  • Asynchronous communication default (Slack, Notion, Loom)
  • No meeting Wednesdays & Fridays for deep technical focus
  • Bi-weekly sprint retrospectives focused on code hygiene
  • Annual company retreat connecting our distributed global team

High Autonomy

We hire senior practitioners and trust them to make architectural decisions. We measure output and software stability—not keystrokes or hours in a chair.

Continuous Mastery

Every team member receives an annual $2,500 learning stipend for conferences, technical books, and advanced cloud certifications.

Sustainable Pace

We don't believe in crunch time or heroic late-night bug firefighting. Proper architectural scoping and automated testing eliminate emergencies.
