Revenue Cycle — Denials & Appeals

Denial Management

A denial is a reason, not a dead claim. The desk groups them, files the ones that should be appealed, and sends the pattern back to the step that caused it.

Request Project Quote Schedule Consultation
Desk Overview

How the work is run

  • CARC and RARC codes grouped so the same denial is worked once
  • An appeal packet includes the chart, not only a letter
  • Filing deadlines are on the claim before the window closes
  • Repeat denials go back to coding, auth, or eligibility
The Operating Problem

Reworking the same denial is how the book stalls

Teams lose the appeal window when every denial is opened as if it were the first one. Grouping by reason shows which denials need a letter and which need a change in how the claim was built.

Scope & Deliverables

What this desk takes on

The work is a queue with an owner, a note, and a next action. These are the pieces that queue contains.

Denial intake by reason code

CARC and RARC codes land in a queue instead of a mailbox.

  • Grouped by payer and by reason
  • A dollar total on each group
  • Duplicates closed so the claim is not appealed twice

Appeal packets

The letter cites the reason and attaches the page that answers it.

  • The operative note, auth, or EOB named in the packet
  • A cover sheet with the claim number
  • A due date taken from the payer’s appeal window

Timely filing watch

A denied claim still has a clock.

  • Filing and appeal deadlines on the worklist
  • A rush queue inside ten days of the limit
  • Claims that cannot be appealed are written off with a reason

Root cause, not only rework

The third denial for the same reason is a process problem.

  • Coded back to documentation, auth, eligibility, or a duplicate
  • The pattern sent to the desk that can stop it
  • A monthly list of the reasons that returned

Payer-specific appeal paths

Each payer’s portal, fax, and form is on the claim.

  • The correct address, not last month’s
  • Status checked after the packet is sent
  • A second level opened only when the first is denied

A record of what was sent

Anyone can see which denial was appealed and what was attached.

  • The packet stored on the claim
  • The decision written back when it returns
  • Underpayments handed to posting, not left as denials

Methodology

How a day on this desk runs

Four steps, kept visible, so a claim does not sit between two people.

Sort the denial

The reason code, payer, dollars, and deadline are recorded, and duplicates are closed.

Decide the path

Appeal, correct and resubmit, or write off. The choice is written on the claim.

Build and send

The packet goes out with the pages that answer the reason, and the send date is stored.

Close the loop

The decision is posted, and a repeating reason is handed to coding, eligibility, or charge entry.
