The Business Challenge

Ending Frustrating Phone Trees and Long Hold Times

Traditional IVR systems frustrate customers and waste valuable human agent time on repetitive triage. Our conversational voice agents listen, understand natural speech, and resolve inquiries in real time.

Scope & Deliverables

What We Build & Deliver

Every engagement produces concrete, documented, and production-tested deliverables.

Ultra-Low-Latency Audio Pipelines

Sub-500ms voice-to-voice response times using optimized streaming STT and TTS.

  • A voice reply that starts before the caller finishes waiting
  • Streaming speech so the line does not go quiet
  • A test call recorded against your real script

Telephony & PBX Integration

Direct SIP trunking and WebRTC connections to Twilio, Asterisk, and enterprise call centers.

  • The agent sits on the numbers you already publish
  • Call routing into Twilio, Asterisk, or the current call center
  • A fallback number if the agent cannot take the call

Dynamic API Action Calling

Voice agents query internal databases live to confirm bookings, check status, or verify identity.

  • The agent can check a booking, a balance, or an order while on the line
  • Only the actions you approve are available to the voice agent
  • A failed lookup is said plainly instead of invented

Seamless Human Handoff

Intelligent escalation with full call summaries routed directly to human specialists.

  • The caller moves to a person without repeating the story
  • The specialist sees a short summary and the last answers
  • After-hours calls can take a message or book a return call

Scripts and the lines it must not cross

The agent follows the wording you approve, including what it should refuse.

  • A script for the main call types
  • Phrases that must transfer to a person
  • A recording of a test call against that script

Hours, overflow, and after the call

The line still behaves when the office is closed or the queue is full.

  • Opening hours and a closed message
  • Overflow to a person or a voicemail
  • A summary stored against the caller
